01 — Problem Statement

The Challenge
We Solved

01

Background

Small business owners often struggle with promoting deals and discounts in a simple, digital way. Many are not highly technical, so creating and sharing professional-looking coupons can feel intimidating. This project explores a digital coupon web app built specifically for owners like Cleef Coffee's — where the process is: log in, create a coupon, preview it, and share it in just a few clicks.

02

The Core Problem

Local cafés, salons, and small shops rely on word of mouth or basic social media posts to attract customers. But designing a coupon that looks professional and distributing it effectively can be frustrating without the right tools. Owners need a simple, step-by-step solution that removes technical barriers while keeping things quick and efficient.

Business Type
Local Cafés & Shops

Physical small businesses that serve walk-in customers and rely on community connections and local marketing.

Technical Literacy
Basic App Users

Comfortable with WhatsApp and Facebook, but not with design or marketing tools — needing a guided, intuitive flow.

Distribution
Digital & In-Store

Coupons shared via messaging apps, social media, or by generating a QR code that can be printed and placed in-store.

Layout Decisions

A brief overview of how each page's layout was planned based on UX research, user behavior, and content priority.

Log In & Sign In Pages

Centered single-column layout to reduce cognitive load. Only essential fields visible — no distractions. Brand logo and warm tone to create trust from first interaction.

Dashboard Page

Left sidebar navigation for persistent access. Top area shows performance summary cards. Main content area highlights active coupons with prominent "Create Coupon" CTA.

Create Coupon Page

Step-by-step form layout with only essential fields: name, discount, expiry. Live preview panel on the right updates in real time, giving owners confidence before sharing.

Share & History Pages

Share options presented as clear, tappable cards: QR code, WhatsApp, direct link. History page uses a table view sorted by recency with quick-access performance metrics.

06 — Usability Testing

Testing &
Iterations

The primary objective was to evaluate how easily small business owners — especially those with little technical experience — could navigate and accomplish key tasks within the app.

Test Setup

  • 5 participants — coffee shop owners and small business operators
  • Remote moderated test using the Figma prototype
  • Conducted in a natural environment at each participant's own pace
  • Simulated a typical small business workflow on laptop

Tasks Given

  • Log into the web app as a returning user
  • Create a 15% discount coupon with an expiry date
  • Share the coupon via a QR code to a customer
  • Check the analytics dashboard or coupon history

⚠ Key Findings

  • 3 out of 5 users didn't immediately notice the "Create Coupon" button — it wasn't visually distinct enough on the dashboard
  • 2 users were unsure where to find coupon performance data for previously created coupons
  • All users enjoyed the warm color palette but suggested action buttons should stand out more prominently
  • Some users hesitated at the sharing step, unsure which option to choose first

✓ Improvements Made

  • Enlarged the primary CTA button and added a descriptive label: "+ Create New Coupon"
  • Added a shortcut "History" button on the dashboard for faster access to past coupons
  • Improved contrast between action buttons and background to increase visual visibility
  • Added step indicators and tooltips during form completion to reduce hesitation and confusion
07 — Final Outcome

The Result

After several rounds of planning, designing, and refining, the coffee coupon web app evolved into a simple yet powerful tool for shop owners. The final design makes it easy to create, customize, and share coupons in just a few steps — all from a clean, intuitive dashboard. Built with the owner's needs in mind, the experience feels efficient and user-friendly, helping businesses grow customer loyalty without any extra effort.

Reflections

Key Learnings

01

Simplicity Is Strategy

Removing unnecessary steps and options isn't lazy design — it's the right call for non-technical users. Every removed friction point became a conversion win.

02

Brand Tone Drives Adoption

The warm cream-and-orange palette wasn't just aesthetic. It made owners feel comfortable — like the app understood their world, not just their workflow.

03

Test Early, Test Often

Usability testing revealed critical CTA visibility issues that prototyping alone missed. Real users in real context caught things no design review could predict.
