01 — Problem Definition

Why Do People
Abandon Habits?

Many people want to improve their daily routines — exercising regularly, studying consistently, building healthier habits. Maintaining these over time is the hard part.

Background

Many people want to improve their daily routines, whether it is exercising regularly, studying consistently, or building healthier habits. However, maintaining these routines over time can be challenging without a system that supports motivation and progress tracking.

The Problem

Most users struggle to stay consistent because they forget to track their habits, lose motivation after missing a few days, or find existing habit-tracking apps overly complicated. Without a simple and engaging system, users often abandon their habits before they become part of their routine.

01

03

04

Key Insights From Research

Users Forget To Track

Many people start habits with motivation but forget to log them daily. Smart, timely reminders are a critical retention feature that most apps underinvest in.

Apps Feel Complicated

Users prefer a clean and minimal interface that does not require too many steps to log a habit. Complexity is the number one reason people abandon habit trackers.

Motivation Drives Consistency

Progress visualization, streak tracking, and motivational reminders measurably help users stay consistent. Visual feedback is not a nice-to-have — it is core.

User Interviews

To understand how people currently manage their habits and where they struggle, I conducted informal user interviews using online discussions and feedback from productivity communities and social media platforms.

Key Questions Asked

  • What makes it difficult for you to stay consistent with habits?
  • Have you used a habit tracker before? What problems did you face?
  • What would make a habit tracking app more useful for you?
  • How do you currently track your daily progress?

What I Found

  • Many users mentioned forgetting to track habits or losing motivation after missing a few days.
  • Several users said existing apps felt complicated or required too many steps to log a habit.
  • Users wanted simple tracking, reminders, and clear progress visualization to stay motivated.
  • Personalized onboarding would help users feel the app was built for their goals.

01
Finding
Habit creation felt slightly long — users expected a faster way to add habits without going through multiple steps.
Improvement Made
Simplified the layout and reduced unnecessary inputs to make habit creation quicker and more streamlined.
02
Finding
Progress insights were useful but not immediately clear — some users needed a moment to understand charts and data.
Improvement Made
Improved visual hierarchy and added clearer labels to make progress data easier to read and interpret.
03
Finding
Users relied heavily on the home screen — most preferred completing actions directly from the dashboard.
Improvement Made
Prioritized key actions and made the home screen more action-focused, reducing the need to navigate away.
04
Finding
Navigation was easy but could be more intuitive — a few users hesitated when switching between sections.
Improvement Made
Refined navigation icons and improved labeling to provide better clarity and reduce hesitation.
05
Finding
Motivation increased with visual feedback — users responded positively to progress indicators and completion feedback.
Improvement Made
Enhanced visual feedback with better progress rings and highlights to make achievements more rewarding.
